# Register additions: AGR-1 irradiation conditions (task research-agr1-irradiation)

These entries are written in the format of `STRATO_Parameter_Register.md` and are NOT yet merged into it. They describe the new files `data/irradiation/agr1_irradiation_conditions.csv` (506 rows, ids IRR-001 to IRR-506), `data/irradiation/agr1_burnup_by_cycle.csv` (2028 rows, ids IRRB-0001 to IRRB-2028) and `data/irradiation/agr1_power_history_digitised.csv` (744 rows, ids IRRP-0001 to IRRP-0744). Row identifiers, values, evidence classes and statuses are those of the CSV files; the CSV files are the source of truth. Evidence classes and statuses use the existing vocabulary of Section 0.2 only: `measured`, `calculated`, `specification`, status `verified` or `digitised`. No new class is introduced. A proposal for one additional optional label is in the report (stage-b/btcs/research-agr1-irradiation.md, Section 7).

Reading guide for the evidence classes in these files. `calculated` in the compact, capsule and power-history rows means "output of the INL as-run neutronics (JMOCUP/MCNP/ORIGEN) or thermal (ABAQUS) analysis"; it is not a measurement and the sources print no uncertainty for the neutronics values. `measured` marks as-manufactured characterisation means. `specification` marks pre-irradiation specification or plan values. Compact names follow the capsule-level-stack convention of Morris et al. (2016) (for example 3-4-3: capsule 3, level 4, stack 3).

No derived quantity (fission-rate density, number density) is entered as a data row. The derivation is in the report, Section 5, and is labelled calculated there.
